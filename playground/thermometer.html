Word Thermometer · playground

Order the words along the scale, coldest first. The team on turn votes on their phones; the teacher clicks. Right keeps the turn, wrong passes it.

One ladder per team, all climbing the same scale. Each side votes on their own next word — you can see their guess appear in the rung it is aimed at — and a right answer moves that team up. Wrong costs nothing but the time. First to the top wins. Nothing to click: the ladders settle it.
