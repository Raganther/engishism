Connections · playground

Find four groups of four connected words. The team on turn talks it over — their phones vote on the words — then the teacher locks in four tiles and submits. A wrong guess passes the turn. Solve a group to unlock its mini-lesson.

Both teams at once. Each player holds up a share of the four on their phone — two words each on a team of two, one each on a team of four — and a team's picks together are their answer. The first team holding a real group takes it. Nothing to click: the dots show who is holding what, and the board settles it.
