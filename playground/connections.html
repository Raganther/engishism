Find four groups of four connected words. The team on turn talks it over — their phones vote on the words — then the teacher locks in four tiles and submits. A wrong guess passes the turn. Solve a group to unlock its mini-lesson.

Both teams at once. Each player holds up to four words on their phone; a team's picks together are their answer, and the first team holding a real group of four takes it. Nothing to click — the board settles it.
