## Target language by section (quick map for game authoring)

- **5A — Crime & punishment:** crime & justice vocabulary (fraud, assault, tax
  evasion, bribery, corruption; convicted, custody, testimony, alibi, verdict);
  legal roles; forms of punishment/rehabilitation; **grammar: relative clauses**.
- **5B — Job requirements & fair pay:** employment vocabulary (sectors,
  qualifications, redundancies, benefits, terms & conditions); noun/verb stress
  pairs; **grammar: obligation, necessity & permission** (must / have to /
  should / be required to / be obliged to / be permitted to / be forbidden to).
- **5C — Recall & speculate (Everyday English):** recalling and speculating
  language (if my memory serves me correctly, I'd hazard a guess, it's slipped
  my mind…); temporary states; main-stress pronunciation.
- **5D — Opinion essay writing:** essay structure; linking for addition and
  reinforcement (in addition, as well as, moreover, besides, furthermore);
  social media & recruitment topic.
