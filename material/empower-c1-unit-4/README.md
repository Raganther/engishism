## Target language by section (quick map for game authoring)

- **4A — That little voice in your head:** instinct & reason vocabulary
  (rational, on impulse, subconsciously, weigh up, on a whim, deep down, gut,
  hunch, objective…); noun phrases; doubt & uncertainty language.
- **4B — He got himself locked in a shed:** structures with *have* and *get*
  (causative / receiving end); memory collocations (vague, painful, vivid,
  photographic, distant, lasting memory); eyewitness / false-memory vocabulary.
- **4C — I see where you're coming from:** being tactful in formal discussions
  (soften criticism / opinions); idioms; homophones & connected speech.
- **4D — So what was her breakthrough moment?:** profile-article writing; time
  expressions (shortly, immediately, gradually, subsequently, the instant,
  before long, it wasn't until…); organising information.
